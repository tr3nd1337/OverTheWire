# OverTheWire

Welcome to my little corner of Linux-induced suffering.

This repository is where I document my journey through the [OverTheWire](https://overthewire.org/) wargames. The goal is simple: learn new things, break stuff (legally), solve problems and hopefully become a better security professional along the way.

Some challenges take five minutes. Others make you question whether you actually understand Linux. That's part of the fun.

## What is this?

OverTheWire offers a collection of wargames designed to teach cybersecurity concepts through practical challenges.

I'll be working through different games, documenting my solutions, mistakes, discoveries and anything worth remembering.

No blindly copying walkthroughs. The idea is to understand *why* something works, not just which command gets the job done.

## How I document things

Each challenge gets its own little write-up.

I'll generally include:

- **The objective:** What am I actually trying to achieve?
- **The investigation:** What did I look at first?
- **The approach:** Which commands did I use and why?
- **The solution:** How did I get there?
- **The takeaway:** What did I learn?

I'll also document mistakes where they're worth mentioning. They're usually where the interesting lessons happen.

Passwords and challenge credentials are kept out of the repository.

## A few ground rules

1. Understand the command before running it.
2. Don't blindly copy solutions.
3. Break things in controlled environments.
4. Document what you learn.
5. If something doesn't work, figure out why before throwing another 15 commands at it.
